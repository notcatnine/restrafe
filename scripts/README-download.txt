Restrafe
========

A single-player reconstruction of STRAFTAT's movement, weapons, HUD, cosmetics, audio and settings, in one test map
with a training dummy. Restrafe contains no STRAFTAT files: it reads your own installed copy of STRAFTAT, so you must
own STRAFTAT on Steam and have it installed. Restrafe is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers or
publisher of STRAFTAT.

Play
----
1. Extract this folder anywhere you can write to (for example your Desktop or Documents; not Program Files).
2. Double-click Restrafe.exe.
   - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the exe is not code-signed: click "More info", then
     "Run anyway".
   - The first launch takes a little longer (about 15-30 seconds) while Restrafe indexes your STRAFTAT files; the
     window can stay dark meanwhile. Later launches take a few seconds.
3. If Restrafe says it cannot find STRAFTAT, double-click setup.exe and paste your STRAFTAT folder
   (Steam > STRAFTAT > Properties > Installed Files > Browse).

Controls
--------
WASD move, Space jump, Shift sprint, C or Ctrl crouch / slide, Q / E lean, F pick up / drop, left / right mouse
button fire the right / left hand, right mouse button aim, mouse wheel swap hands, 1-0 voice lines,
Escape settings and pause menu.
Weapons menu: ` (backquote, the key to the right of Backspace). Lists every weapon; click one to spawn it in front
of you. Press ` again or Escape to close.
Cosmetics menu: K. Hats, suits and smoke, with a 3D preview. Close with OK, K or Escape.

Settings are saved in the config folder next to Restrafe.exe.
