**You need STRAFTAT installed through Steam.** Restrafe contains no STRAFTAT files; it reads your own copy.

### Play
1. Download `Restrafe-…-windows-x64.zip` below and extract it (Desktop or Documents, not Program Files).
2. Double-click `Restrafe.exe`. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the exe is not code-signed): click
   **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The first launch takes about 15-30 seconds while Restrafe indexes your STRAFTAT files.
4. If it cannot find STRAFTAT, run `setup.exe` and paste your STRAFTAT folder.

Controls and details are in `README.txt` in the zip and in the [README](https://github.com/notcatnine/restrafe#readme).
Windows 10/11 64-bit; tested with STRAFTAT build 25333005. This is an early release: please report problems as
[issues](https://github.com/notcatnine/restrafe/issues).
